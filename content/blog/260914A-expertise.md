I've been a big fan of the old adage _never take criticism from someone you wouldn't take advice from_. It has helped me tremendously in filtering out the signal from the noise on social media and in real life.

This current wave of AI-doomerism online made me realize it's time to add a second one: _never take warnings of collapse from someone who can't explain what's holding it up_.
